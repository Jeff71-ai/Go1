❓ How to Play

🎯 Create Mode

  • Choose board size (2-19) and capture delay (0.5-3 seconds)
  • Left click: Black, Right click: White (or use the toggle buttons)
  • Record the correct solution (Black first, alternating). Full Go rules apply.
  • Set viewer permissions: allow view solution, allow territory estimate
  • Generate a share link to share with others

🎮 Play Mode

  • Black plays first, alternating with White
  • Wrong moves trigger an alert and reset the puzzle
  • Correct solution triggers confetti animation and records your time
  • Captured stones turn orange, then explode and fade away

⚔️ VS Mode (Chinese Rules)

  • 2-player local match with full Go rules
  • Captured stones turn orange → explode into particles + gentle capture sound
  • Ko rule: cannot immediately recapture. Ko point marked with red dashed circle.
  • Territory estimate: real-time area and score comparison (toggleable)
  • Game ends after two consecutive passes. Scored by Chinese rules (komi: 3.75)

📐 Chinese Rules

  • Area scoring: stones + surrounded empty points
  • Black gives 3.75 komi (7.5 points equivalent)
  • Game ends when both players pass consecutively
